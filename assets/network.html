Inside a Soothsayer Forecast

One training sample. It covers P = L + S positions, the L lags the model looks back on and the S steps it forecasts, and B samples train at once. The letters in the shapes are sizes set by your config: C changepoints, K Fourier terms of a period, E event window columns, R future regressors, Lr lag columns over all lagged regressors, Q quantiles, N series. Without auto-regression L = 0, P = 1 and the middle region does not exist.

input tensor built by fit dense layer, in → out features elementwise op only with some configurations edges carry tensor shapes. One color per component.

Everything with a dashed outline depends on the config: the logistic saturation on growth: :logistic, the × trend step on multiplicative seasonality, events or regressors, the ReLU nets on ar.layers, a regressor's layers and lagged_regressors_layers, the series one-hot on series.column, and the whole quantile region on quantiles.
